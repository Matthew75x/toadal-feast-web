 HOW TO PLAY

Feed Gulper

1 · TIME THE CLAW

The claw sweeps automatically. Tap anywhere in the play area, or press Space/Enter, to act.

2 · FEED & GROW

Center grabs earn PERFECT. Food grows Gulper; favorites score more. A clean streak multiplies score.

3 · CLEAR VS MASTER

In Campaign, hit the meal goal to clear. PERFECT, streak, and favorite targets are star bonuses. Advanced modes require every listed goal.

4 · READ THE CABINET

Hearts heal. Bombs hurt. Shrink syringes erase growth. Jeweled Candies give temporary powers.

5 · MASTERY I · CHALLENGE

Prove the base one-tap cabinet under speed, clocks, hazards, precision, and gauntlet rules.

6 · MASTERY II · REAL GAMER

Track moving Gulper across a wide arena: first tap grabs, second tap releases. Hazards can be dumped safely.

7 · MASTERY III · LONG DROP

Lead moving Gulper through a 620-unit fall. Your first clear includes a subtle landing guide; after that, prediction is fully yours.

8 · LEARN FROM MISSES

Mastery runs briefly show timing error after a delivery so you can correct the next release instead of guessing.
