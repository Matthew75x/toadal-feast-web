# Visual System and Content Rules

Strict visual authority: `03_VISUAL_AUTHORITY/00_APPROVED_HOME_VISUAL_AUTHORITY.png` plus Batch 1 references.

## Visual language
- lush TOADAL FEAST food-fantasy environments;
- warm cream content cards over colorful illustrated environments;
- dark chocolate/brown navigation;
- pink primary CTAs;
- gold/crown reward accents;
- polished illustrated canonical characters;
- premium playful game UI;
- dense and feature-rich, but organized and legible;
- environmental framing with food, foliage, waterfalls, floating islands, whimsical architecture;
- TOADAL GAMES studio identity small/subordinate except on About/Business contexts.

## Canonical Toadal
Golden-yellow frog/toad, small crown, red scarf, explorer/adventurer identity. He is King of Feasts, mischievous/confident/adventurous, not a generic prince and not a yellow reskin of the classic green frog.

## Navigation authority
Top-level concept: Home | Play | World | Stories | Media | Feast Pass | App | Community | Store
Support/Search/Account/Settings can be utility controls or secondary nav.

## Do not
- do not create generic SaaS/corporate white-card layouts;
- do not replace Toadal with unrelated frog designs;
- do not make the studio catalog more visually dominant than TOADAL FEAST on the main experience;
- do not pretend unfinished backend/social/store systems are live;
- do not silently delete historical good features;
- do not recreate the full mobile game as a web game.
