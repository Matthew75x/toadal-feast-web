## Identity and presentation

- Primary TOADAL FEAST franchise identity, subordinate TOADAL GAMES studio identity.
- Chocolate navigation, cream/parchment panels, pink primary CTAs, navy headings and gold accents.
- Rich food-fantasy surroundings; compact portal composition and useful content density.
- Canonical golden-yellow Toadal with crown, red scarf and established anatomy, overlaid separately from the environment.
- Responsive route-specific composition; readable phone layout rather than a scaled desktop image.
- Genuine product screenshots, accessible text/controls and optimized assets.

Do not use the Android app icon as the website wordmark or let generated mockup characters redefine canon. [Mascot guardrails](../design/MASCOT_IDENTITY_GUARDRAILS.md) and [asset policy](../implementation/ASSET_INTEGRATION_POLICY.md) retain the detailed rules.

## Acceptance scope

Written composition acceptance, automated checks, screenshots and owner visual acceptance are different evidence. The WO-001 written-composition PASS did not claim a pixel comparison to a then-missing Home image; WO-002 recovered the exact approved image. Current staging is useful visual progress, not approval of every page in the 30-page product plan.

Art approval does not establish feature availability. Empty editorial libraries, disabled store destinations, missing progression and a static reaction image must remain visible gaps even when their layouts or truth labels are correct.
