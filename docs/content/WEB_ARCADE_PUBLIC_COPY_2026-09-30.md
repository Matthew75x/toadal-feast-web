## Product label

**TOADAL FEAST Arcade Preview**

Short descriptor:

> A compact TOADAL FEAST Arcade sampler built for the web.

Do not call it the full game.

## Experience cards

### Standard Arcade

**Catch. Survive. Chase your best.**

Play the core Arcade loop with a small web roster. Start with Toadal, then unlock Classic Frog and Gully through web-preview progression.

Primary action: **Play Arcade**

### 5 Minute Feast

**Five minutes. One high score.**

Chomper races the clock in a five-minute score sprint. Misses do not use Standard Arcade's ordinary miss penalty.

Primary action: **Start 5 Minute Feast**

### Zen

**A gentler way to feast.**

Play as Princess Lily and clear the buried kingdom room by room. Zen removes Standard miss damage and focuses on steady catch progress.

Primary action: **Enter Zen**

## Standard character cards

### Toadal

**King of Feasts**

Build Golden Charge by eating, then use Toadal's Royal Hop, tongue, Golden Throw and Golden Block.

State: **Unlocked**

### Classic Frog

**The classic catch**

A straightforward frog built around familiar movement and tongue catching.

Locked copy: **Complete your first Arcade run**

### Gully

**Take to the air**

Glide in four directions, scoop food in flight and build bigger combo scores from consecutive catches.

Locked copy: **Reach 600 best score or complete 3 Arcade runs**
